Our Product

Xael.io - An immersive dark RPG browser game that will captivate your imagination

Xael.io

Dive into the mystical world of Xael.io, where strategy meets dark fantasy. Deploy mystical spiders into battle to harvest Shadow, create Dark Elves, and consume their energy to grow stronger, evolve, and dominate the arena.

Play Xael.io Now

Summon Familiars

Discover and summon mystical familiars from across different realms. Each familiar brings unique abilities and characteristics that will aid you in your strategic conquests.

Produce Shadows

Master the ancient art of shadow production. Gather ethereal energy and forge shadows that serve as the foundation for creating powerful mythological transformations.

Transform Creatures

Witness the incredible metamorphosis as your shadows transform into rare mythological creatures. Each transformation unlocks new powers and strategic possibilities.

RPG Gameplay

Engage in deep RPG gameplay that challenges your tactical thinking. Plan your moves carefully as you build your collection and compete with other players.

Browser-Based

Enjoy seamless gaming directly in your browser. No downloads required - jump into the action instantly from any device with an internet connection.

Multiplayer Experience

Connect with players from around the world. Share strategies, trade creatures, and compete in tournaments to prove your mastery of the celestial arts.
